# The print station

Polis prints the pairings and the place cards of every published round, by
itself, on a printer in the hall. On request it also prints the standings, a
player list in alphabetical order with board and room (the sheet for the
door), and result slips, one per board, four to a page. You ask from the
station, from the tournament's Print station page, or from the Hall card in
[Manubrium](/help/manubrium-app); the player list and the slips can also be
switched on to print with every new round. It is a small program on a computer with a
printer that joins a tournament in OpenPairings with a join link and keeps a
line open to it. It prints each round once, and again only when somebody
asks. It always dials out, so nothing in the hall's network has to be
opened.

**When you need it.** When the pairings should be on the wall without anybody
at the arbiter's laptop pressing Print, or when a hall has more than one
room: a station can print only one room's boards, some board numbers or some
categories. A round changed after it was printed is never reprinted by
itself; the station says so and offers Reprint.

**Where it fits.** In OpenPairings, the station is added under the
tournament's Settings, *Print station*, *Add a print station*. The link it shows is good once,
for fifteen minutes: paste it into Polis on the station's computer, or scan
its QR code with [Manubrium](/help/manubrium-app) on your phone, which passes
the link on (copy or share) to that computer. Rooms are set
in [Creating a tournament and its settings](03-tournament-setup.md#rooms-page). For
printing by hand, see [Printing](09-printing.md). Its sister in the hall is
[Nunki](/help/other/nunki), which reads the same rounds aloud.
